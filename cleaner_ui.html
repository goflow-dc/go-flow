Go Flow

Version

Go Flow keeps your Windows laptop clean, fast and safe: it removes junk, finds big and duplicate files, frees memory, and works with your antivirus and Windows Update.

Everything runs on this laptop. Go Flow has no account, no ads and no tracking, and it never sends your files or data anywhere on its own. The internet is only used when you ask Windows to check for updates or new virus definitions, or when you choose to check a file with VirusTotal (only after you add your own free API key in Settings — only a fingerprint of the file is sent, unless you explicitly choose to upload it). Nothing is deleted without your confirmation.

Threat check

Looks for signs of trouble a virus scan can miss: protection switched off, odd things starting with Windows, disguised files, a changed hosts file. It only looks and reports. It works alongside your antivirus and does not replace it.

Large files

The biggest files in your folders (system and app folders are skipped). Ticked files go to the Recycle Bin, so you can still restore them.

Duplicate files

Files with exactly the same content, checked byte by byte. The oldest copy is kept by default; extra copies go to the Recycle Bin.

Downloads tidy-up

Setup files and zip files in your Downloads folder that you have not opened for a while. Setup files can be downloaded again; zips that are already unzipped are not needed. Ticked files go to the Recycle Bin. Photos and documents are not listed.

Startup apps

Apps that start with Windows. Turning one off works like Task Manager: the app stays installed and you can turn it back on any time.

Weekly auto-clean

Once a week Windows runs a safe clean on its own: temp files, browser caches (only if the browser is closed), crash reports and thumbnails, then updates Defender and runs a quick scan. It never empties the Recycle Bin and never touches large files, duplicates or your folders. If the laptop is off at that time, it runs the next time you switch on.

Restore points

A restore point is a Windows "undo" for system changes (drivers, updates, settings). It does not touch your personal files. Windows keeps one new point per day at most.

Alerts

Every hour (and when you sign in) Windows checks quietly and shows a notification if a drive is almost full or virus protection is off. At most one notification a day for each problem.

History

Every clean, scan and change is saved in the logs folder.
